Refund Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

This Refund Policy explains the conditions under which GONAF+ may issue a refund for an order or service request.

1. General Principles

Refunds are reviewed based on the order status, payment received, requested service, and circumstances of the request.

A payment does not automatically guarantee a refund.

Each refund request may be reviewed before a decision is made.

2. Unverified Payment

If a payment cannot be verified, GONAF+ may place the order on hold or reject it.

When a refund is applicable, it may be processed after the payment and the required information have been verified.

3. Rejected Orders

When an order is rejected by GONAF+, a refund may be issued when the payment has been received and there is no legitimate reason for GONAF+ to retain the funds.

The refund timeframe may depend on the payment method used.

4. Incorrect Information

The customer is responsible for the information provided when submitting an order.

If an order fails because of incorrect information provided by the customer, GONAF+ may not be able to provide a full refund.

This may include an incorrect identifier, address, phone number, email address, or any other information required to complete the service.

5. Cryptocurrency Transactions

For cryptocurrency transactions, the customer must carefully verify the address and network before confirming the transaction.

For USDC on Base, the customer must verify that the address is correct, the network is Base, and the asset is USDC.

Blockchain transactions may be irreversible.

Funds sent to an incorrect address, incorrect network, or unsupported asset may be permanently lost and may not be refundable.

6. Orders Already Processed

When a service has already been successfully completed, a refund may not be available.

Refund requests for completed orders will be reviewed on a case-by-case basis.

7. Fees

Depending on the circumstances, certain fees may be non-refundable.

Fees charged by external services, payment providers, or blockchain networks may also be non-recoverable.

8. Refund Method

When possible, refunds may be issued using the same payment method used for the original transaction.

In certain situations, GONAF+ may request additional information before processing a refund.

9. Refund Timeframes

Refund timeframes may vary depending on the payment method, the provider involved, and the verification required.

Once GONAF+ initiates a refund, the time required for the funds to appear may depend on the payment provider or financial institution.

10. Fraud and Unauthorized Activity

GONAF+ may suspend or refuse a refund when a transaction is under investigation for fraud, unauthorized use, money laundering, or other illegal activity.

Additional information may be requested to verify the transaction.

11. Requesting a Refund

To request a refund, the customer may contact GONAF+ using the contact information available on the platform.

The request may require:

- the order ID;
- payment-related information;
- a description of the issue;
- and any additional information required for verification.

12. Refund Decisions

GONAF+ reviews each request based on the available information, the applicable service conditions, and applicable laws.

Approval of one refund does not mean that all future refund requests will automatically be approved.

13. Changes to This Policy

GONAF+ may modify this Refund Policy when necessary.

The last updated date will be changed when significant modifications are made.

14. Contact

For questions regarding this Refund Policy or a refund request, contact GONAF+ using the contact information available on the platform.
