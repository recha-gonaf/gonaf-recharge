Depending on the services you use, we may collect:

- First and last name

- Information necessary to create and manage your account

Transaction Information

When you use our services, we may process:

- Order information
- Transaction amount and currency
- Requested service
- Information necessary to provide the requested service
- Payment method information
- Transaction references or identifiers
- Payment proofs that you choose to submit

Identity Verification Information

When identity verification is required for certain services, we may process the information necessary to complete that verification in accordance with applicable requirements and the terms of the identity verification provider we use.

Technical Information

We may also receive technical information necessary to operate and secure the service, such as:

- IP address
- Device type
- Browser type
- Connection information
- Technical logs and security information

We aim to collect only information that is necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

We may use your information to:

- Create and manage your account
- Process your orders and requests
- Provide requested services
- Verify payments
- Prevent and detect fraud, abuse, and unauthorized activity

- Communicate with you about your account, orders, or services

When you submit a payment proof, it may contain personal or financial information.

Payment proofs may be used to:

- Verify your payment

- Prevent fraud

5. Identity Verification

6. Sharing of Information

We may share certain information when necessary with:

- Technical service providers

- Hosting, security, or infrastructure providers
- Competent authorities when required by law

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or unauthorized disclosure.

We also recommend that users:

- Keep their login credentials confidential
- Use a strong password

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, or when retention is necessary to:
